What led to this

On 30 September 2026, Maj Gen (Rtd) Kahinda Otafiire was in Parliament presenting the Water & Environment sector report. In his maiden speech to the 12th Parliament he said:

“The hallmark of civilization is how people resolve contradiction without violence. When you see a General who prefers violence as the best means of solving problems, then there is a problem. The greatest Generals I know of are those who resolve problems without shooting a bullet.”

He named no one. But the remarks came amid long-running tension with Gen. Muhoozi Kainerugaba over succession politics and the use of force. Two days later Muhoozi responded with the public one-week deadline above.
